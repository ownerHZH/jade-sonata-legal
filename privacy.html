Privacy Policy

Jade Sonata: Mahjong Piano · Luthfi Studio
Effective date: 2026-10-05

This policy explains how Luthfi Studio handles information for Jade Sonata: Mahjong Piano, including the installed Android game and its public website.

Information kept on your device

The installed game stores progress, settings, mistakes, hints, retries, key-press timing, performance takes and optional adaptive-difficulty summaries in app-private storage. These records support saving, replay, scoring and adjusting future levels. Performances contain game events, not microphone recordings.

No gameplay collection or account

This release does not require an account and does not transmit gameplay records to Luthfi Studio. It includes no advertising, analytics SDK, cloud synchronization or connected external AI service. The Android app does not request Internet, microphone, camera, location, contacts, phone, SMS or broad media/storage permissions. Game artwork, level data, music scores, piano samples and these legal documents are included in the installed app.

Exports you choose

You can explicitly export a backup using the system save or share panel. The destination you select receives that file and handles it under its own policies. We do not automatically upload backups. Exported copies outside the app remain under your control and must be deleted separately.

Retention and your choices

Local records remain until you clear app data or uninstall the game. You can disable personalization for future levels or reset its learning without deleting earned progress. Android automatic cloud backup is disabled for this app. Keep your own exported copy if you need a backup; clearing app data can permanently remove local records.

Support correspondence

If you email our support address, we receive the address and information you choose to send and use them to respond and resolve the request. We retain correspondence only as needed for support and applicable obligations. Do not send unnecessary sensitive information. Contact us if you want to ask about or request deletion of information you sent us.

Website and external links

Opening the public website or an external link requires an Internet connection and sends ordinary web-request information, such as an IP address and browser information, to the receiving service and hosting providers. The game website uses OpenAI Sites, and these public legal pages use GitHub Pages. The hosting services handle requests under their own policies. The Web game keeps gameplay saves in your browser and does not upload them through its game code. Clearing browser storage can remove those saves. This website traffic is separate from the installed Android game, which can be played offline.

Security and children

The Android game uses app-private storage and exposes exported files only through user-initiated sharing. No method guarantees protection from device loss or unauthorized access to an unlocked device. We do not request age or knowingly collect children’s gameplay records. A parent or guardian can contact us about information a child has sent through support.

Changes and contact

We will reflect changes in this policy and its effective date. For privacy questions or requests, contact Luthfi Studio at the email address below.

隐私政策

Jade Sonata: Mahjong Piano · Luthfi Studio
生效日期: 2026-10-05

本政策说明 Luthfi Studio 如何处理《琴雀：麻将钢琴》（Jade Sonata: Mahjong Piano）相关信息，适用于已安装的安卓游戏及其公开网站。

保存在设备中的信息

游戏在应用私有存储中保存进度、设置、误触、提示、重试、按键时序、演奏记录及可选的难度适应摘要，用于存档、回放、评分与调整后续关卡。演奏记录是游戏操作事件，不是麦克风录音。

无需账号，不上传游戏记录

本版本无需注册账号，不向 Luthfi Studio 传输游戏记录，不含广告、分析 SDK、云端同步或已连接的外部 AI 服务。安卓应用不申请网络、麦克风、相机、定位、通讯录、电话、短信或广泛的媒体与存储权限。画面、关卡、乐谱、钢琴采样及这些法律文档均随应用安装。

由你选择的导出

你可以主动通过系统保存或分享面板导出备份。你选择的接收方会收到该文件，并按其自身政策处理。我们不会自动上传备份。应用外的导出副本由你管理，需要单独删除。

保存期限与选择

本机记录保留至你清除应用数据或卸载游戏。你可以关闭未来关卡的个性化或重置学习记录，而不删除已获得的进度。本应用已禁用安卓自动云备份。需要备份时请保留自己的导出副本，清除数据可能永久移除本机记录。

支持邮件

当你向支持邮箱发送邮件时，我们会收到你的邮箱地址和你主动提供的信息，并用于回复及处理请求。我们仅在支持处理及适用义务所需期间保留往来邮件。请勿发送不必要的敏感信息。你可以联系我们，查询或申请删除曾发送的信息。

网站与外部链接

打开公开网站或外部链接需要联网，会向接收服务及托管服务发送正常的网页请求信息，例如 IP 地址及浏览器信息。游戏网站使用 OpenAI Sites，公开法律文档使用 GitHub Pages 托管；相关服务按各自政策处理请求。Web 游戏将存档保存在浏览器中，游戏代码不会上传这些存档；清除浏览器存储可能移除记录。网站请求与可离线运行的已安装安卓游戏相互独立。

安全与儿童

安卓游戏使用应用私有存储，仅在用户主动分享时向接收方提供导出文件。任何方式都无法保证避免设备丢失或他人访问未锁定设备的风险。我们不要求填写年龄，也不主动收集儿童游戏记录。家长或监护人可就儿童通过支持渠道发送的信息联系我们。
